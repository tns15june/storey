How well it works

Storey in three minutes

AI plan review for city building departments. Storey reads a permit drawing set, follows every stair from floor to floor, checks what the set declares against what it draws, and drafts the correction letter. It never approves a plan: an examiner makes every decision.

Captions are in the picture. The upload and the stair removal are real runs, sped up, on Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 and SAM 3.1.
