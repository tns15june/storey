Storey video: voiceover script

Read the lines in this order, in one recording.
Leave about 2 seconds of silence between lines, so each line can be cut out and placed on its own scene.
Re-read a line straight away if you stumble; the last take of each line is used.

 1. Storey. First-pass plan review for city building departments, built on Meta's Muse Spark and SAM 3.1.
 2. In Portland, eight in ten permit plans needed corrections. Seattle's own AI pilot passed six point four percent of the checks a human examiner sent back. Storey has no pass state at all.
 3. Upload a permit set. This is The Capitol, a 22-sheet duplex from a public pattern book.
 4. Muse Spark reads every sheet, orders the floors and follows each stair. This real run, sped up 16 times, took 3.3 minutes and cost 0.39 USD.
 5. The result: a draft correction list, with the evidence for every item.
 6. Every stair followed floor to floor. SAM 3.1 tracks each one through the stack and draws it.
 7. No model decides alone. Spark is asked three times, then ink at the place and a blind crop question must agree.
 8. Found means: 3 of 3 runs, ink at the place, and the crop said yes.
 9. Test it live: remove the central stair from the second floor of a copy.
10. Storey runs the stair stage again on the copy. Sped up 20 times.
11. Must fix: Stair C is missing on the second floor. 0 of 3 runs, the crop said no, no ink at the place.
12. Real drafting errors in a published set: this title block names the wrong floor.
13. Each item names the sheet, the place, what was read, what is needed, and why.
14. What Storey could not check is listed too. An unread item never becomes a pass.
15. Houston: one stair cannot serve the fourth and fifth floors of this block.
16. Switch to Seattle. The rules run again in a moment, with no model call, and its single-exit rule removes both items.
17. The resubmittal: each earlier comment is checked again at its place on the new set.
18. Looks resolved, Still open or Cannot tell, with the earlier and new drawing side by side.
19. The comment letter, drafted in the format cities already send. The examiner edits it and decides.
20. Measured on thirty-three sets, two held out until the code was frozen. Storey raised all one hundred and eighty-four generated items, Spark asked directly eighty-one, with ten times the false findings. One wrong pass in sixteen held-out stair cells. And real audits show the next step: more checks.
21. Storey drafts. The examiner decides.
